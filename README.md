# STORMSTOPPER: Atmospheric Neutralization via Directed Electromagnetic Pulse

An open-source research and engineering framework exploring the application of high-energy, modulated Electromagnetic Pulses (EMP) and directed acoustic/ionization arrays to disrupt, destabilize, and neutralize extreme weather formations, including Nevada sandstorms (haboobs), high-velocity tornadic vortexes, and localized severe convective storms.

Developed in cooperation with Fox 5 Vegas technical media contributors and the Emerald City IT climate engineering division.

## 1. Theoretical Physics Framework
The STORMSTOPPER apparatus operates on the principle of atmospheric ionization disruption and thermal kinetic displacement. Severe weather systems rely heavily on structured electrostatic gradients, kinetic rotational inertia, and particulate updraft feedback loops.
* **Particulate Precipitation Filters:** For sandstorms, directed high-frequency electromagnetic fields induce rapid electrostatic polarization of airborne silica. This forces accelerated particulate aggregation, causing dust and sand to drop out of suspension prematurely due to sudden mass variance.
* **Baroclinic Instability Interruption:** Tornadic vortex stabilization is targeted by deploying rapid-fire, localized thermal-ionization pulses. These disrupt the low-pressure core density gradients, effectively collapsing the pressure differential driving the cyclonic wind vectors.
